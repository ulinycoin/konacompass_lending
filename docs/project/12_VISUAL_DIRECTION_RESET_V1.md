# Kona Compass — Visual Direction Reset V1

## Decision
Текущую версию лендинга считать неудачным technical draft и не пытаться спасать косметикой.

## New Direction
Собирать новый лендинг как **premium niche hardware landing** с ощущением:
- serious marine equipment
- cinematic product framing
- dark, restrained, confident
- editorial hierarchy instead of component soup
- fewer but stronger sections

## What to avoid
- pill text
- repeated rounded cards everywhere
- startup-looking CTA clusters
- decorative gradients as main visual idea
- fake proof or placeholder-heavy layout
- section spam without visual hierarchy

## What to lean into
- large product photography
- asymmetrical editorial layout
- high-contrast typography
- clean luxury-industrial feel
- direct conversion path
- proof through image scale, spacing, and restraint

## Visual principles
1. Product is the hero, not UI chrome.
2. One strong action is better than three equal CTAs.
3. White space and image scale carry premium feel.
4. Fewer sections, better composition.
5. Strong contact path without looking cheap.

## Layout strategy
- Hero: large left-aligned editorial headline + dominant product image
- Compatibility: quiet trust row, not loud badges
- Product section: why it matters, with image-led composition
- Feature proof: not a grid of cards, but 2-column text + selective highlights
- Demo/media: one strong proof section
- Contact block: direct, clear, premium

## Tone
Not startup. Not gadget e-commerce. Not generic outdoors brand.
It should feel like a specialized premium tool for people who already understand the category.
