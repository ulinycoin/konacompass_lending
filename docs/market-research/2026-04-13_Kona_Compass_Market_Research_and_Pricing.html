3. Market Structure

Kona Compass sits in the segment of forward-facing sonar accessories / live sonar transducer control systems.

This category includes manual rotators, motorized rotators, automated search systems, aiming systems with foot control, and transducer control accessories integrated with trolling motors or poles.

Customers in this category have already accepted expensive electronics setups. That makes a premium accessory in the $1,000 to $1,500 range commercially plausible.

4. Regional Market Readiness

United States

The USA is the strongest priority market due to adoption density, premium buyer behavior, dealer networks, and influence channels.

Canada

Canada is a strong adjacent market with similar buyer logic and strong relevance for premium predator/walleye electronics fishing.

Scandinavia

Scandinavia is highly promising because of technical buyer maturity, predator fishing culture, and fit with rugged engineering-led positioning.

Broader Europe

Europe is viable, but fragmented. Practical expansion order: USA, Canada, Scandinavia, Germany/Netherlands/UK, then broader Europe.
