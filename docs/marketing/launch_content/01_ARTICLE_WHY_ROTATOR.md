## 1. The "Visual Guess" Problem
When you rotate a transducer manually, you are guessing the angle. A few degrees of error at the surface translates to missing the target by several feet at depth.

**The Kona Compass Fix:** We’ve built a high-precision heading-based system that provides accurate positioning. You don't guess the direction; you know exactly where it's pointed, every single time.

## 2. The Friction of Constant Correction
In manual setups, every time the boat drifts or the wind shifts, you have to take your hand off the reel to adjust the transducer. This friction kills your reaction time.

**The Kona Compass Fix:** With **5 specialized operating modes** (including Auto-Scan and Heading Hold), the system does the heavy lifting for you. Use the wireless remote or the control pedal to make micro-adjustments without breaking your fishing flow.

## 3. Durability in Rugged Conditions
Most "affordable" rotators on the market are built with plastic parts and exposed wiring. They fail when you need them most—in heavy rain or rough water.

**The Kona Compass Fix:** Kona Compass is housed in a **fully sealed marine-grade casing**. It’s built to survive the vibration of high-speed runs and the harsh marine environment. It’s not a gadget; it’s a professional tool.
